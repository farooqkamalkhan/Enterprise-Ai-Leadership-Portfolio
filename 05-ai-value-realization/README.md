# 💰 AI Value Realization

### Connecting AI investment to measurable enterprise outcomes

![AI Value Realization](../05-ai-value-realization.png)

AI value realization requires more than estimating benefits in a business case. It requires explicit ownership, baseline measures, financial validation, adoption evidence, and recurring review of **planned versus realized value**.

---

## Benefit Validation Model

| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| **Business Owner** | Owns outcome, workflow change, adoption, and benefit realization |
| **Finance Validator** | Challenges assumptions, validates methodology, and confirms material financial benefits |
| **Executive Sponsor** | Owns strategic alignment, removes barriers, and remains accountable for the investment decision |

Technology and AI teams enable delivery, but the business outcome cannot be outsourced to the delivery team.

---

## Value Categories

AI can create value through **growth, hard-dollar cost reduction, cost avoidance, productivity/capacity, cycle-time reduction, quality, risk reduction, customer/member experience, employee experience, and improved decision quality**.

The value type should be explicit because **time saved is not automatically cash saved**. Capacity benefits require a defined mechanism for redeployment, avoided hiring, throughput, service improvement, or another measurable outcome.

---

## Measurement Lifecycle

**Define Outcome → Establish Baseline → Assign Owners → Measure Adoption & KPI Movement → Validate Realized Outcomes → Compare Planned vs. Realized Value → Expand / Adjust / Stop**

---

## Executive Value Reporting

**Investment → Delivery Stage → Adoption → KPI Movement → Realized Value → Risk → Decision**

This distinguishes between AI that has been **deployed**, AI that is **being used**, and AI that is actually **changing enterprise performance**.

The objective is a portfolio where investment decisions can be defended with evidence and successful capabilities receive the resources to scale.
