# ⚡ Enterprise AI at Scale

### Selected leadership case studies

These case studies illustrate experience scaling AI, machine learning, conversational systems, and AI-ready data capabilities across large enterprise environments. They are intentionally presented at an outcome and leadership level and exclude confidential architecture, source code, proprietary data, and employer-sensitive information.

---

## Case Study 1 — Enterprise GenAI Capability

**Context:** Finance teams needed faster access to knowledge and more efficient handling of high-volume analytical and support work.

**Leadership focus:** Connected business use cases, platform strategy, adoption, operating mechanisms, and measurable outcomes across a large technology portfolio.

**Selected outcomes:**
- Scaled AI-enabled capabilities to **15,000+ users**.
- Reached **70%+ active monthly adoption within 90 days**.
- Reduced case summarization time by approximately **75%**.
- Reduced tax research time by approximately **60%**.

**Leadership lesson:** Enterprise GenAI value comes from combining the model with trusted knowledge, workflow integration, adoption, and outcome measurement.

---

## Case Study 2 — Conversational AI & Intelligent Intake

**Context:** Users needed a simpler way to navigate support and enterprise services without understanding complex routing structures.

**Leadership focus:** Helped scale a conversational intake and support capability using NLP, intent modeling, workflow automation, and Jira integration.

**Selected outcomes:**
- Scaled to **20,000+ users**.
- Improved support resolution speed by approximately **40%**.
- Reduced more than **30 hours per week** of manual workload.

**Leadership lesson:** The highest-value conversational experiences do more than answer questions; they understand intent, retrieve trusted knowledge, route work, preserve context, and improve the underlying operating model.

---

## Case Study 3 — ML Decision Intelligence

**Context:** Global business teams required earlier and more accurate forecasting signals.

**Leadership focus:** Supported the scaling of machine-learning forecasting across **12 global business units**, connecting data readiness, business adoption, and decision processes.

**Selected outcomes:**
- Improved forecast accuracy by **18–22 percentage points**.
- Enabled business decisions approximately **2–3 weeks earlier**.

**Leadership lesson:** Predictive capability creates value when the output changes a decision, not merely when the model produces a more accurate prediction.

---

## Case Study 4 — AI-Ready Data Foundations

**Context:** Analytics and AI delivery were constrained by fragmented data discovery, quality, access, and governance.

**Leadership focus:** Advanced data catalog, lineage, quality scoring, role-based access, master-data practices, and self-service analytics as foundations for scalable AI.

**Selected outcomes:**
- Reduced data sourcing and preparation effort by approximately **20%**.
- Increased analytics delivery by approximately **40%**.
- Improved data-management efficiency by approximately **20%**.
- Reduced remediation effort by approximately **35%**.

**Leadership lesson:** AI readiness is often a data operating-model problem before it is a model problem.

---

## Scale Pattern

Across these cases, the recurring leadership pattern is:

**Business Outcome → Data & Technology Foundation → Governed Delivery → Adoption → Measurement → Scale**
