# 1. AI & Intelligent Systems

## GenAI in Finance

**Challenge →** Finance teams needed faster access to knowledge and more efficient analytical and support workflows.

**Leadership Decision →** Treat GenAI as a governed enterprise capability rather than a collection of isolated experiments—aligning business use cases, platform strategy, Legal, Security, governance, and executive adoption.

**What We Built →** Enterprise GenAI capabilities supporting automated case summarization, tax research, financial analysis, and LLM-enabled variance analysis.

**Scale / Outcome →** **15,000+ users**, **70%+ active monthly adoption within 90 days**, approximately **75% faster case summarization**, and approximately **60% faster tax research**.

**Why It Mattered →** Established a repeatable model for moving emerging AI from experimentation into governed, adopted enterprise capability.

## OneBot | AI Intake & Support

**Challenge →** Fragmented support channels created manual routing, inconsistent user experiences, and unnecessary workload.

**Leadership Decision →** Build intelligence into the intake architecture through conversational interaction, intent modeling, personalization, and workflow integration rather than layering AI onto a legacy process.

**What We Built →** Conversational intake and support capability using NLP, intelligent routing, workflow automation, and Jira integration.

**Scale / Outcome →** **20,000+ users**, approximately **40% faster support resolution**, **85%+ first-contact resolution for common queries**, and more than **30 hours/week** of manual workload reduced.

**Why It Mattered →** Demonstrated how conversational AI can become an operating-model layer connecting users, knowledge, routing, and enterprise workflows.

## Intelligent Business Growth | ML Forecasting

**Challenge →** Global business teams relied heavily on backward-looking financial reporting and manual forecasting.

**Leadership Decision →** Move ML recommendations into business decision workflows rather than confining predictive capability to back-office analytics.

**What We Built →** ML-driven forecasting and recommendation capability supporting demand planning, financial projections, and performance prediction.

**Scale / Outcome →** Deployed across **12 global business units**, improving forecast accuracy by **18–22 percentage points** and enabling decisions approximately **2–3 weeks earlier**.

**Why It Mattered →** Shifted forecasting from reporting what happened toward anticipating what leaders should do next.

# 2. Data & Analytics Foundations

## Finance Data Factory | Walmart U.S. Digital

**Challenge →** Fragmented data sourcing and redundant pipelines slowed analytics and AI delivery.

**Leadership Decision →** Align Product, Engineering, and Finance around a reusable platform-first data architecture and trusted source of financial intelligence.

**What We Built →** Foundational consumer and financial data platform supporting analytics, forecasting, reporting, and AI model training.

**Scale / Outcome →** **10,000+ users**, approximately **20% less data sourcing/preparation effort**, **6+ redundant pipelines eliminated**, **40% faster analytics delivery**, and reuse across **8 downstream AI and analytics programs**.

## Finance Data Factory | Sam's Club

Unified Merchandising and Finance data domains to support shared forecasting and cross-domain analytics. Outcomes included approximately **35% less reconciliation time** and elimination of **4+ manual reporting handoffs per planning cycle**.

## International Data Foundation | Daily Sales

Standardized governed daily sales intelligence across international markets, currencies, and reporting dimensions. Reduced data latency from approximately **48 hours to same-day for 12+ markets** and improved international performance monitoring.

## Finance Data Hub | Self-Service Analytics

Created a governed self-service analytics environment with catalog, role-based access, query and visualization capabilities. Scaled to **15,000+ users**, reduced dependency on centralized reporting teams by approximately **50%**, moved common insight delivery from weeks to hours, and reached **80%+ adoption within six months**.

# 3. Finance Transformation

## OneStream | Global Enterprise Performance Management

**Challenge →** Fragmented planning environments constrained global consistency and slowed Finance processes.

**Leadership Decision →** Align domestic and international Finance, Engineering, and vendor partners around a unified global planning platform.

**What We Built →** Enterprise Performance Management environment supporting planning, forecasting, and ML-enabled insight across Walmart U.S., Sam's Club, and international Finance.

**Scale / Outcome →** Consolidated **5+ legacy planning systems** and reduced monthly close cycle time by approximately **25%**.

## Digital FP&A

Modernized planning toward continuous, insight-driven performance management through real-time data, scenario modeling, and collaborative workflows. Outcomes included approximately **30% shorter annual planning cycles** and transition toward rolling forecasts.

## Digital P&L | Sam's Club

Replaced static post-close reporting with automated, mobile-accessible financial intelligence. Reduced report delivery from hours to near real-time and expanded decision access across field leadership.

# 5. Operations, Automation & Governance

## Data Governance Platform

Repositioned governance as an enabler of trusted analytics and AI. Advanced catalog, lineage, quality scoring, role-based access, and MDM practices. Outcomes included approximately **20% higher data-management efficiency** and **35% lower remediation effort**.

## Nexus Hub | One-Timer & FX Central

Automated high-friction Finance workflows, replacing manual email and SharePoint processes while preserving auditability and controls. Reduced one-time adjustment processing by approximately **60%** and improved real-time FX visibility.

## Walmart U.S. Fleet

Advanced certified financial and operational data supporting fleet cost, maintenance, performance, and logistics decisions—moving leadership from periodic reporting toward proactive operational intelligence.

## Enterprise Asset Management

Modernized asset lifecycle visibility across acquisition, depreciation, maintenance, and disposal to improve Capex decisions, capitalization accuracy, and audit readiness.
