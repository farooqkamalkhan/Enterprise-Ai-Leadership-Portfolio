# 🏛️ JPMorgan Chase | Regulated Technology Transformation

### Decisioning systems, servicing-platform migration, regulatory compliance, customer communications, and nationwide delivery

This portfolio captures selected transformation leadership during a period of intense mortgage-industry regulatory change. The work built an early foundation in **enterprise decisioning, systems-of-record migration, regulatory execution, customer communications, operational risk, and large-scale delivery**—capabilities that later became highly relevant to governed AI and enterprise transformation leadership.

# 1. HAMP Agent Desktop Conversion

**Challenge →** Federal Home Affordable Modification Program policy changes required rapid updates to mortgage-modification decisioning while preserving accuracy, auditability, and compliance.

**Leadership Decision →** Treat policy interpretation, underwriting rules, calculation logic, testing, Compliance, Legal, and operational readiness as one integrated release.

**What We Built →** Updated underwriting decisioning capability incorporating HAMP-aligned eligibility rules, calculation logic, and audit trails.

**Outcome →** Delivered policy-aligned decisioning against federal timelines while maintaining underwriting accuracy and audit readiness and reducing reliance on manual overrides.

**Why It Mattered →** Established experience translating evolving public policy into governed enterprise decision systems under regulatory pressure.

---

# 2. VLS → MSP | Home Equity Servicing Migration

Led enterprise release management for migration of the Home Equity portfolio from a legacy servicing environment to **Black Knight LoanSphere MSP**.

The transformation encompassed **data conversion → parallel validation → reconciliation → cutover → post-conversion stabilization**, requiring alignment across Technology, Servicing Operations, Default, Risk, Compliance, external platform integration, and data-conversion teams.

**Strategic Value →** Reduced legacy platform risk and established reusable migration patterns for subsequent servicing transformations.

---

# 3. MetLife → MSP | Acquisition Integration

Led release management for migration of an acquired loan portfolio into the JPMorgan servicing environment. The work connected acquisition integration, data transformation, platform conversion, reconciliation, exception handling, compliance, and borrower continuity.

**Strategic Value →** Demonstrated how to balance acquisition urgency with platform integrity, regulatory requirements, and uninterrupted servicing operations.

---

# 4. Loss Mitigation Decision Letter Redesign

Directed design, content strategy, technical integration, variable-population logic, trigger logic, testing, and rollout monitoring for regulated borrower communications.

**Outcome →** Maintained regulatory disclosure timelines through rollout, reduced defects and ambiguity, and improved the clarity and reliability of borrower-facing decisions.

**Strategic Value →** Connected customer experience, workflow engineering, data accuracy, Compliance, Legal, and operational execution in a highly scrutinized domain.

---

# 5. Dodd-Frank REG-X + HUD Regulatory Portfolio

Led parallel regulatory releases aligning enterprise systems, policies, procedures, communication logic, and workflows to evolving **Dodd-Frank / REG-X and HUD requirements**.

**Outcome →** Delivered required enterprise upgrades on regulatory timelines and maintained continuous compliance through complex federal policy changes.

**Strategic Value →** Built deep credibility operating where technology delivery, legal interpretation, policy, risk, and operational continuity intersect.

---

# 6. Default Workflow System | Nationwide Deployment

Led cross-functional Agile delivery and end-to-end UAT strategy for nationwide loss-mitigation workflow deployment, including defect management, sprint health, risk mitigation, executive reporting, and rollout readiness.

**Outcome →** Supported nationwide deployment without regulatory compliance gaps while maintaining executive visibility into delivery health and operational risk.
