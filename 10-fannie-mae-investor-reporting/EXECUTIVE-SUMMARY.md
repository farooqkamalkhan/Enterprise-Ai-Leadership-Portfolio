# Fannie Mae | Executive Case Study

## Investor Reporting & Servicer Data Modernization

**Transformation thesis:** Replace a legacy manual reporting model with standardized, governed, system-driven reporting while protecting data integrity and operational continuity across a nationally significant servicing ecosystem.

| Executive Lens | Case Study |
|---|---|
| **Business Need** | Modernize legacy call-in investor reporting and reduce manual operational risk |
| **Ecosystem Complexity** | Thousands of mortgage servicer partners plus internal Product, Technology, Reporting, Servicing, Risk and Compliance teams |
| **Leadership Focus** | Integration testing, release management, reconciliation, exception handling and operational readiness |
| **Capability** | Automated servicer data submission, standardized interfaces, reconciliation controls and governed investor reporting |
| **Strategic Outcome** | Eliminated the legacy call-in process and improved reporting accuracy, timeliness and consistency |

---

## Modernization Flow

**Legacy Call-In Reporting → Automated Data Submission → Standardized Interfaces → Reconciliation → Exception Management → Governed Investor Reporting**

### What This Demonstrates

- Modernizing a systemically important process across a large external partner ecosystem.
- Treating data migration, testing, operations and governance as one transformation problem.
- Protecting business continuity while replacing legacy processes.
- Leading change where accuracy, timeliness and control are non-negotiable.

### Leadership Principle

> **Enterprise modernization succeeds when technology change and operating-model change are designed as one system.**
