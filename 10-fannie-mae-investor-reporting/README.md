# 🏠 Fannie Mae | Investor Reporting & Servicer Data Modernization

### Replacing legacy manual reporting with governed, system-driven integration

This case study highlights transformation leadership across **investor reporting, servicer data, enterprise integration, testing, release management, operational risk, and external-partner change**.

> **Portfolio note:** Presented at a generalized leadership and outcome level without confidential implementation detail or proprietary data.

### [📌 Executive Case Study at a Glance →](EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md)

---

## Executive Challenge

A legacy call-in reporting model depended on manual processes across a large mortgage-servicing ecosystem. Modernization required more than a system change: it required coordinated transformation across reporting interfaces, data submission, reconciliation, exception handling, internal operations, technology, and external servicer partners.

---

## Leadership Role

Led the **integration test and release-management workstream** for the strategic change initiative, partnering across Product, Technology, Investor Reporting Operations, Servicing Operations, Risk, Compliance, and external servicer integration teams.

The objective was to protect reporting integrity and operational continuity while moving a nationally significant reporting process toward automated, system-of-record-driven execution.

---

## Transformation Model

**Legacy Call-In Reporting → Automated Servicer Data Submission → Standardized Interfaces → Reconciliation Controls → Exception Management → Governed Investor Reporting**

---

## Enterprise Scope

| Dimension | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | Mortgage servicing / investor reporting |
| **External Ecosystem** | Thousands of mortgage servicer partners |
| **Operating Model** | Internal Product, Technology, Reporting and Servicing Operations + external integrations |
| **Control Focus** | Accuracy, reconciliation, exception handling, timeliness, operational risk |

---

## Outcomes

The transformation eliminated the legacy call-in reporting process, improved reporting accuracy and timeliness, standardized data submission across the servicer ecosystem, and reduced operational risk through automated reconciliation controls.

### Strategic Value

Modernized a critical mortgage-industry process by replacing manual reporting with **governed, automated, system-driven investor reporting at national scale**.

### Executive Leadership Lesson

Large-scale modernization succeeds when technology migration, external ecosystem change, testing rigor, operational readiness, and governance are treated as **one transformation problem rather than separate workstreams**.
