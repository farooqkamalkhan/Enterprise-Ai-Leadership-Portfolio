# Wells Fargo | Executive Case Study

## Digital Wealth Transformation — Intuitive Investor

**Transformation thesis:** Create a digital advisory capability that could compete with emerging automated-investing experiences while operating within the integration, governance, and regulatory realities of a Tier 1 financial institution.

| Executive Lens | Case Study |
|---|---|
| **Business Need** | Expand digital investing and modernize the advisory experience |
| **Enterprise Complexity** | Four wealth and trust lines of business plus Product, Digital, Engineering, Operations, Risk, Compliance and Legal |
| **Leadership Focus** | Cross-LOB alignment, program governance, integration, executive communication, regulated delivery |
| **Capability** | Digital onboarding, risk profiling, goal-based investing, automated portfolio construction and ongoing portfolio management |
| **Strategic Outcome** | Established a competitive digital advisory presence and reusable digital wealth patterns |

---

## Transformation Flow

**Customer Need → Digital Onboarding → Risk Profile → Goal-Based Strategy → Automated Portfolio Construction → Advisory Experience → Ongoing Management**

### What This Demonstrates

- Leading transformation across organizational boundaries rather than inside one product silo.
- Connecting customer experience to enterprise architecture and operating-model realities.
- Working effectively across Product, Engineering, Operations, Risk, Compliance and Legal.
- Delivering digital innovation inside a highly regulated environment.

### Leadership Principle

> **A digital experience is only enterprise-ready when product strategy, technology, operations, governance, and adoption move together.**
