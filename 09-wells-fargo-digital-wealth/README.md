# 🏦 Wells Fargo | Digital Wealth Transformation

### Intuitive Investor | Cross-LOB digital advisory platform

This case study highlights leadership experience at the intersection of **digital product transformation, wealth technology, cross-line-of-business integration, governance, and regulated financial services**.

> **Portfolio note:** Presented at a generalized leadership and outcome level. No confidential architecture, customer data, or proprietary implementation detail is included.

## Executive Challenge

As digital investing accelerated, a Tier 1 financial institution needed to expand beyond traditional advisory models and create a competitive digital wealth experience while operating within the controls, integration complexity, and stakeholder environment of a large regulated bank.

---

## Leadership Role

Served as a senior technology program leader supporting the development of **Intuitive Investor**, aligning stakeholders across **Wells Fargo Advisors, Institutional Retirement & Trust, Corporate Trust, and Investment Management**.

The leadership challenge was broader than delivery: create alignment across distinct businesses, technology teams, operations, Risk, Compliance, and Legal around a common digital advisory experience.

---

## What Was Built

An integrated digital wealth capability combining:

**Digital Onboarding → Risk Profiling → Goal-Based Investing → Automated Portfolio Construction → Advisory Experience → Ongoing Portfolio Management**

The platform connected customer-facing digital experiences with the governance and operating requirements of multiple wealth and trust organizations.

---

## Enterprise Scope

| Dimension | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Industry** | Wealth Management / Financial Services |
| **Enterprise** | Tier 1 U.S. financial institution |
| **Business Integration** | 4 wealth and trust lines of business |
| **Stakeholders** | Product, Digital, Engineering, Advisory Operations, Risk, Compliance, Legal |
| **Audience** | Retail and mass-affluent investors plus internal advisory organizations |

---

## Why It Mattered

The program established a digital advisory presence during the industry's shift toward automated investing and created reusable patterns for **digital onboarding, integrated wealth experiences, and automated portfolio construction**.

### Executive Leadership Lesson

Digital transformation inside a regulated enterprise requires more than a compelling customer experience. Success depends on connecting **product strategy, technology integration, operating-model alignment, risk controls, and adoption across organizational boundaries**.
